# Welcome to The Fellowship

We're three houses next to each other in Travis Heights, sharing one backyard. It's a bit like renting a mansion together, if the mansion had three wings and a lot more privacy.

<!-- truncate -->

## The Property

There are 12 bedrooms, three kitchens, several living rooms, a conservatory, a loft, and more than half an acre of land. It's quiet and green here, and still close to everything. Downtown, Town Lake, South Congress and East Austin are all 3 to 10 minutes by car.

## Amenities

- **1 to 5 Gbps fiber**, so remote work just works
- **The Conservatory**, a big glass room for hanging out, working or music
- **Hot tub and cold plunge**
- **Theatre room** with a projector and screen for movie nights and karaoke
- **A shared self-driving Tesla**
- **Barbell gym**, fully set up on-site
- **Backyard chickens** in a mobile coop
- **Guest loft** for visitors
- **Woodwork shed** with tools for projects
- **Fire pits and a grill** for nights outside

## The Community

We're a healthy, considerate bunch: grad students, artists and people in tech. We throw parties, put on the odd performance, have band practice, host tea parties and salons, watch movies, do karaoke, and sit down to regular "family dinners."

Join in as much or as little as you like. There's always somewhere quiet if you'd rather be alone.
