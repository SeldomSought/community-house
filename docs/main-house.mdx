# Main House

Main House sits in the middle of the property, and a lot of house life ends up happening here.

## Key Features

### Outdoor Propane Grill & Range
A built-in propane grill, good for barbecues and cooking outside. Someone cooks, everyone else hangs around and offers advice.

### Fire Pit
There are a few fire pits around the yard. Evenings out here usually mean s'mores, long conversations, or just looking up at the sky.

### Backyard Chickens
The chickens live in a mobile coop and lay pretty much every day. Breakfast is a few steps from your door, and the eggs are better than anything from the store.

### Coworking Space
A room set up for focused work, and for working alongside other people when you'd rather not be alone. Fast fiber, comfortable chairs and good light.

## Living Spaces

- Roomy bedrooms in a few different layouts
- A big kitchen made for cooking together
- A central living area that opens onto the backyard
- The half-acre yard right outside
