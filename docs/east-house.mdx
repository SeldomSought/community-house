# East House

East House is where you can sleep, cook, work and train without leaving the building.

## Key Features

### Bedrooms
Comfortable bedrooms with newer finishes.

### Kitchen
A full kitchen with everything you'd need.

### Coworking Space
A second room set up for work, with fast fiber and a desk that's yours to use when you need to focus.

### Barbell Gym
A full barbell setup in the garage. No membership and no drive. You just walk out and lift. Here's what's in it:

- Olympic barbell and plates
- Squat rack
- Bench press
- Dumbbells and kettlebells
- Pull-up bar

If you want to go from a lift into cold water, the cold plunge is over at West House, next to the hot tub.
