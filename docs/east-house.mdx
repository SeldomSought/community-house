# East House

East House is where the lifters tend to end up. The gym and the cold plunge are both right here.

## Key Features

### Barbell Gym
A full barbell setup in the garage. No membership and no drive. You just walk out and lift. Here's what's in it:

- Olympic barbell and plates
- Squat rack
- Bench press
- Dumbbells and kettlebells
- Pull-up bar

### Cold Plunge
There's a cold plunge from Plunge out on the patio. People use it after a lift, or first thing in the morning when coffee isn't cutting it.

If you like going hot then cold, the hot tub is over at West House.

## Living Spaces

- Comfortable bedrooms with newer finishes
- A full kitchen with everything you'd need
- A living room set up for watching things
- Its own patio

## The Fitness Lifestyle

If you live in East House, training stops being a thing you have to fit in. You get up, you lift, you get in the cold water, you go to work. Nothing in between to talk you out of it.
