# West House

West House has a lot of the things people end up loving about this place, inside and out.

## Key Features

### The Conservatory
A big room with glass walls. People gather here, work here, practice music here, or just sit and enjoy the light. It opens straight onto the backyard, so it feels half outdoors.

### Hot Tub
The hot tub is where a lot of days end. Good for sore muscles after the gym, and even better for late conversations under the stars.

### Guest Loft
A loft set aside for visitors. Friends and family can come stay without taking over anyone's living room, and they get a bit of privacy too.

### Theatre Room
A proper projector and screen for movie nights, watch parties and karaoke. Comfortable seats and a good sound system.

## Living Spaces

- Bedrooms with shared or private bathrooms
- A full kitchen with up-to-date appliances
- A cozy living room with lots of natural light
- A door straight out to the shared backyard
