## Shared Spaces

Everyone who lives here can use all of this:

**Indoor**
- **The Conservatory**, a big room for hanging out, working or practicing music
- **The sunroom**, a bright room for meals and slow mornings
- **Theatre room** with a projector, a screen and a sound system, for movies and karaoke
- **Guest loft**, kept free for visitors
- **3 kitchens**, one in each house, all fully stocked
- **Several living rooms**, so there's always a couch free

**Outdoor**
- **A half-acre backyard** with room for parties
- **Hot tub and cold plunge**, side by side
- **Fire pits** in a few different spots
- **A built-in propane grill**
- **Chickens** in a mobile coop, with eggs to match
- **Woodwork shed** with tools and room for projects

**Fitness & Wellness**
- **Barbell gym**, fully set up
- **Hot tub** for the evenings
- **Cold plunge** for the mornings you need waking up

**Tech & Connectivity**
- **1 to 5 Gbps fiber** in every house
- **A shared self-driving Tesla**
