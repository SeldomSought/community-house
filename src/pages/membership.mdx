## Utilities

Utilities usually come to **$100 to $150 per person** a month. That covers electricity, water, gas and trash.

If you're arriving with a suitcase and not much else, we can partly furnish your room.

---

## What's Included

Every room comes with the run of the place:

- **1 to 5 Gbps fiber**, in all three houses
- **The Conservatory**, a big glass room for dinners, work or music
- **Hot tub and cold plunge**, side by side
- **Theatre room** with a projector, a screen and real speakers. Movie nights and karaoke happen here.
- **A shared self-driving Tesla** for errands and getting around
- **Barbell gym** with no membership to pay
- **Backyard chickens** in a mobile coop, so there are usually eggs
- **Guest loft** for when people come to visit
- **Woodwork shed** with tools and space for projects
- **Half an acre outside** with fire pits, a propane grill and room for parties
- **Three kitchens**, fully stocked, one per house
- **Several living rooms**, so there's always somewhere to sit

## The Community

We're a healthy, considerate bunch. Grad students, artists, people who work in tech. Some of what tends to happen around here:

- Parties and the odd performance
- Band practice and jam sessions
- Tea parties, salons and long arguments about ideas
- Movie nights and karaoke
- Regular "family dinners"

Join in as much or as little as you like. There's plenty of quiet space if you're more of an introvert.

---

## Location

Travis Heights is quiet and green, and still close to everything:

- **3 to 10 minutes** by car to downtown, Town Lake, South Congress and East Austin
- An easy ride by bike or scooter
- Trails and neighborhood spots you can walk to
