# Travis Heights, Austin

The Fellowship is three houses side by side in Travis Heights, sharing one backyard. It's a quiet, leafy street, and you can still be downtown in a few minutes.

## What's Nearby

**Downtown Austin**, 3 to 10 minutes
- Live music and nights out
- Tech companies and startups
- Restaurants and bars

**Town Lake (Lady Bird Lake)**, 3 to 10 minutes
- More than 10 miles of hiking and biking trails
- Kayak and paddleboard rentals
- Running paths and outdoor workouts

**South Congress (SoCo)**, 3 to 10 minutes
- Local boutiques and vintage shops
- Austin restaurants everyone's heard of
- Live music

**East Austin**, 3 to 10 minutes
- Galleries and artist studios
- Breweries and coffee shops
- Food trucks, and so many tacos

---

## The Property

Half an acre, and most of it is yard:

- Three houses next to each other, joined by the backyard
- A few different places to sit outside, with fire pits and a grill
- Room to throw a real party
- Chickens in a mobile coop
- A lot of trees
