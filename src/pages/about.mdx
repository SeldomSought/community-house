We're three houses next to each other in Travis Heights, sharing one backyard. It's a bit like renting a mansion together, if the mansion had three wings and a lot more privacy.

## The Community

We're a healthy, considerate bunch. Grad students, artists, people who work in tech.

### What We Do Together

Things that tend to happen around here:
- Parties and the odd performance
- Band practice and jam sessions
- Tea parties, salons and long talks about ideas
- Movie nights and karaoke
- Regular "family dinners"

Join in as much or as little as you want. There's always somewhere quiet if you'd rather be alone.

---

## Amenities

What you get with any room:

- **1 to 5 Gbps fiber**, so remote work is a non-issue
- **The Conservatory**, a glass-walled room for dinners, work or music practice
- **Hot tub and cold plunge** right on the property
- **Theatre room** with a projector and screen for movies and karaoke
- **A shared self-driving Tesla** for errands and getting around
- **Barbell gym** with no membership to pay
- **Backyard chickens**, and the eggs that come with them
- **Guest loft** for visitors
- **Woodwork shed** with tools and space to build things
- **Outdoor kitchen** with a built-in propane grill and fire pits

---

## Location

Travis Heights is quiet and green, and still close to everything:

- 3 to 10 minutes by car to downtown, Town Lake, South Congress and East Austin
- Easy by bike or scooter
- A walkable neighborhood with plenty of trees
